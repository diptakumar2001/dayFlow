Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026

DayFlow is a free daily planner made as a student coursework project. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it never sells or shares your data.

Without an account

Everything stays on your device: your tasks, settings and background photos. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account

  • Stored online: your email address, your tasks and your settings, so they can sync between your devices. They're kept in a Supabase database. Each account can only read its own rows (row-level security).
  • Your password is handled by Supabase's sign-in service, which stores only a scrambled (hashed) version. DayFlow never sees or stores it.
  • Emails: you only get emails you ask for: confirming your address and resetting your password.
  • Background photos never leave your device, even with an account.

Reminders and alarms

Reminders are scheduled on your device by your phone or browser. DayFlow doesn't use push servers.

Backups

"Save a backup" writes a file to your device. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: open the account menu and choose Delete account. This removes your login, tasks and settings from the server straight away.
  • On a device: clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.
